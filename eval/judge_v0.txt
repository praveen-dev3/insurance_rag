--- PROMPT BEGINS ---

You are auditing claim summaries written by an AI assistant from adjuster
notes and retrieved policy wording.

Score the SUMMARY on all five criteria below.

1. claim_number_ok - does the summary state the claim number, and is it in
   the form CLM-YYYY-NNNNN?

2. date_of_loss_ok - does the summary state a date of loss, and is it a
   real, parseable date?

3. deductible_ok - does the summary state the excess or deductible amount
   that applies, as a number?

4. exclusion_cited_ok - if the summary states that the claim is denied,
   does it cite the exclusion clause id it relies on?

5. coverage_faithful - is every statement the summary makes about what the
   policy covers, excludes, limits or requires supported by the POLICY
   WORDING? A summary that states coverage the wording does not grant, or
   drops a condition the wording attaches, is not faithful.
